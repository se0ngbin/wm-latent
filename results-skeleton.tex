\section{Results}

\paragraph{Models (naming used throughout).}
\begin{itemize}
  \item sigreg: LeWM default (SIGReg). jac: + encoder-Jacobian Frobenius norm pinned to 1 (sigreg sits at $\approx 10$).
  \item jac+adv$_h$: + adversarial $L_\infty$ ($8/255$, 2 PGD steps) invariance of the safety readout $h$ (MLP adversary head).
  \item jac+adv$_z$: + same adversary, but attack and penalty on the whole latent $\|f(x+\delta)-f(x)\|^2$ (no head).
  \item ``varying radius'': $\varepsilon$ log-uniform in $[4/255, 32/255]$ per training sequence.
  \item Pull variants (CBF section only): jac+pull (predictor action-separation, target 1), jac+pull+adv$_h$.
  \item Other world models (comparison only, \texttt{dreamerdino.md}): SafeDreamer (RSSM; plain and + jac) and DINO-WM (frozen DINOv2 encoder), same data, margin functions and CCEM rule; Dubins and Reacher (SafeDreamer), Dubins (DINO-WM).
  \item Margin function $h$: MLP 192-512-512-1 or linear $w^\top z+b$, gradient-penalty margin loss, trained on frozen latents of real + imagined states (real-only is an ablation). $h\ge 0$ = safe.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Planners and protocols.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Raw CEM = LeWM CEM (300 samples, 30 elites; Dubins 30 iters, Reacher 10 iters). CCEM = feasible iff $h>0$ on current + every imagined state; fallback = highest $\min_k h(\hat z_k)$.
  \item Dubins: goals $K$ steps out whose straight line crosses an obstacle; main $K=10$, secondary $K=15$; open loop; 50 eps/seed. OOD: purple obstacles, 90° rotation (test-only).
  \item Reacher: invisible joint-space box; held-out start/goal pairs 25 env steps apart whose recorded path enters the box; frameskip 5, 5-block horizon, replan every 5 blocks; 50 pairs (broad table) or 150 paired episodes (key comparisons, exact McNemar).
  \item OGBench cube: CEM \todo{protocol, shifts}.
  \item Dubins CBF: frozen LeWM $\to$ $h$ $\to$ reachability critic (DDPG in imagination) $\to$ CBF-filtered diffusion policy, 1000 trajectories. Filter needs only current frame + 1 predicted step.
  \item \todo{state seeds per benchmark: Dubins sigreg/jac/jac+pull/jac+pull+adv$_h$ 3 seeds; Dubins jac+adv$_h$ 1 seed; all Reacher models 1 seed (jac s1, varying-radius s1 training)}
\end{itemize}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Claim (Table~\ref{tab:q1_transfer}): jac keeps $h$'s ranking under every appearance and geometric shift that breaks sigreg (Dubins purple 0.970 vs 0.543, rotation 0.984 vs 0.637; Reacher floor 0.2 / exposure / white balance 0.998--0.999 vs 0.34--0.66), with no in-distribution cost; on Reacher it keeps the threshold too (sign acc 0.980--0.991).
  \item Other world models: each fails on some Dubins shift, jac on none (ranking). SafeDreamer loses colour and rotation; DINO-WM (frozen DINOv2) keeps them but its shape threshold collapses (sign acc 0.500). On Reacher SafeDreamer's $h$ is as robust as jac's; jac's advantage there is in planning (Q2). jac on SafeDreamer does not fix colour / rotation (0.496 / 0.619; target 1 does not transfer to the RSSM encoder).
  \item Generality beyond these: CartPole and Safety-Gym Point (perception only) show the same pattern for colour (Point: sigreg at chance on unseen layouts, jac 0.953 base / 0.733 colour) (Appendix~\ref{app:transfer}).
  \item Why it works --- mostly invariance (Table~\ref{tab:q1_mech}). With the encoder Jacobian pinned to 1 (sigreg sits at $\approx 10$), intensity-type shifts move jac's latent 8--20$\times$ less (Reacher floor 0.2 / light / white balance 0.08--0.14 vs 1.41--1.68 of the latent spread; Dubins diamond 0.14 vs 1.10; PGD 0.10--0.11 vs 1.5--1.6), so states move only 0.02--0.11 of a class gap along the safety axis instead of 0.6--1.2. In units of real motion: at Reacher floor 0.1 sigreg's latent moves 1.6$\times$ as far as 5 env steps of motion, jac's 0.06$\times$ (reacher-results Table 6).
  \item Not compression, on Reacher: class gap / spread and motion / spread are unchanged (0.58 vs 0.56; 0.59 vs 0.60), so jac rejects nuisance while keeping state resolution. On Dubins jac does halve the class gap relative to the spread (0.377 vs 0.756): safety is drawn in the image, so capping pixel sensitivity also caps the safety signal.
  \item Rotation is the exception to ``invariance'': jac's latent moves almost as far as sigreg's (1.37 vs 1.62 of the spread), but nearly orthogonally to the safety axis ($\cos$ 0.07 vs 0.24) and differently per state (shared-offset share 0.06), so states move only 0.27 of a class gap. jac reaches rotation robustness through orthogonality, not invariance.
  \item Colour (Dubins purple) is where neither route suffices: the shift still moves states 0.95 of a class gap along the safety axis (sigreg 1.44), so the threshold breaks (sign acc 0.500) while the scale-free separation survives (post-oct-21 Table 1: $d'$ ratio 0.57 vs 0.15) --- hence ranking without threshold. Colour carries safety on Dubins.
  \item The limit is local (sets up Q3): on Reacher jac's latent shift jumps from 0.19 to 1.27 of 5-step motion between floor 0.2 and 0.3, exactly where its $h$ breaks (floor 0.3: 0.77 of the spread, 0.40 of a gap along the axis).
  \item Linear $h$ is the more robust readout (Dubins purple jac 0.970 vs 0.648 MLP; Reacher camera shift 0.860 vs 0.506 sign acc), consistent with a mostly linear safety axis.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Constrained CEM.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Claim (both benchmarks): without a safety term sigreg and jac collide about equally OOD; the red-trained $h$ cuts jac's OOD collisions and does nothing for sigreg.
  \item Dubins $K=10$: CCEM-MLP purple collision jac 14\% vs sigreg 37\% (raw 40 / 37); rotation 14\% vs 48\%. In distribution CCEM keeps jac at 7--8\%. $K=15$ in Table~\ref{tab:dubins_k15} (appendix): 32 vs 71 (purple), 33 vs 56 (rot.); jac beats sigreg on all 3 seed pairs ($p\le 0.024$). \todo{paired McNemar at $K=10$}.
  \item Reacher: sigreg's $h$ calls everything safe under any photometric shift, so its CCEM \emph{is} raw CEM; jac stays at 0.04--0.08 under every photometric/sensor shift.
  \item Why: CCEM can only veto what $h$ flags. If the encoder moves OOD states across $h$'s boundary, the constraint becomes vacuous (sigreg). If invariance keeps states put, the in-distribution $h$ still applies (jac).
  \item Other world models (Dubins $K=10$, protocol-unmatched): CCEM collisions are comparable (purple 13--20\%, rotation 20--23\% vs jac 17\% / 23\% with linear $h$); jac's edge is success (red 92\% vs 82--83\%) and cost (DINO-WM planning $\approx 2$ GPU-h per 50 episodes). SafeDreamer + jac is shift-invariant but at a worse operating point ($\approx 80$ / 22 everywhere). Claim to make: robust without a pretrained encoder, at a fraction of the planning cost --- not ``more robust than DINO-WM.
  \item Other world models (Reacher): jac plans more safely than SafeDreamer under every photometric/sensor shift (CCEM collision 0.04--0.08 vs 0.09--0.21) and at floor 0.3 (0.10--0.14 vs 0.34--0.49), although SafeDreamer's $h$ is as robust (Q1): the gap is in the planner (goal cost, $h$ precision along plans).
  \item Dynamics shifts: no model differs; safety is limited by the planner/world model, not perception. Brief mention only.
  \item OGBench cube (Table~\ref{tab:cube}): \todo{results pending}.
  \item \todo{Fig: collision vs.\ goal distance $K\in\{10,15,20\}$ for sigreg / jac, red / purple / rot., raw vs.\ CCEM.}
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{CBF safety filter (Dubins).}
\begin{itemize}
  \item In distribution, jac-base filters work (jac gpimag 0.6\% collision, jac+pull 0.8\%, at $\approx$ 0.79 success); sigreg is at or above no-filter collision with every margin function. SafeDreamer's filter: 3.1\%.
  \item Why sigreg fails even in distribution: $h$ is accurate on real frames (buffer AUC 0.983) but the critic is trained in imagination; on imagined latents sigreg's gpfail $h$ is false-safe 0.968 (jac+pull 0.240).
  \item Imagined-state training (gpimag) is the best margin function on jac-base models: imagined false-safe gpfail $\to$ gpimag: jac+pull 0.240 $\to$ 0.093, sigreg 0.968 $\to$ 0.302.
  \item Pull: jac+pull $\approx$ jac with MLP margins (buffer 1.6\% vs 7.1\%, gpimag 0.8\% vs 0.6\%). \todo{say whether pull is kept; pull target 1 was never tuned.}
  \item Negative: adv$_h$ on top hurts the CBF in distribution with every margin (jac+pull+adv$_h$ gpfail 10.0\% vs 2.4\%, gpimag 2.1\% vs 0.8\% at lower success). Linear margins block the goal (success 0.16--0.52).
  \item OOD (Table~\ref{tab:cbf_ood}, appendix) follows the same reach story as CEM: diamond (in reach) works for jac / jac+pull (3--4\%) and SafeDreamer (2.9\%), not sigreg (43\%); purple (out of reach) gives no-filter collision for every model incl.\ SafeDreamer (51\%).
  \item \todo{Fig: example CBF trajectories, jac vs sigreg, red / diamond / purple.}
\end{itemize}

  \item Account: reach $\times$ headroom. Reach = how much the shift moves $h$ (median $|\Delta h|$ / safe--unsafe gap) for that model; headroom = jac must be hurt enough to collide. Alignment of the shift with the adversary's directions does not predict gains (0.17--0.43 for every shift).
  \item Reacher fixed radius: adv$_h$ halves camera-shift collision at equal safe success (0.427 $\to$ 0.207, $p<0.001$). Under floor shifts it is a more conservative operating point (less goal reaching): $h$ is invariant, the goal latent is not. Whole latent (adv$_z$) also protects the goal cost: camera shift 0.427 $\to$ 0.160 with higher safe success.
  \item Varying radius extends reach and that predicts planning: varying adv$_h$ floor 0.4 0.340 / 0.100 $\to$ 0.087 / 0.653 ($p<0.001$ both), floor 0.3 $\to$ 0.040 / 0.673, occlusion (50 pairs) 0.24 / 0.44 $\to$ 0.10 / 0.62; no in-distribution cost. Within a shift, unsigned reach ranks the models for floor and camera shift ($\rho$ 0.60--1.00 at any depth) but not tilt (0.10--0.40); the signed current-frame shift does (0.90). So: measure reach where the planner reads $h$, and keep the sign. (\texttt{reach\_corr.py} numbers, not yet a paper claim: 5 points per shift.)

Predictor tracks purple as well as red, but $h$'s safe/unsafe gap in the purple region is $\approx 30\times$ smaller, so prediction noise erases the ranking.   \item Reacher differs: OOD the latent blurs near the boundary (50\% veto point 0.05--0.1 rad $\to$ 0.25 rad for jac at floor 0.3) but imagined plans keep a usable ranking; $h$ errs optimistic, so CCEM degrades to raw CEM. \end{itemize}

%=====================================================================
\section{Discussion (bullets)}
\begin{itemize}
  \item jac: needed for any safety constraint (CEM or CBF) to survive appearance shift on LeWM; robust across all three Dubins shifts where SafeDreamer and DINO-WM each fail one; matches DINO-WM's planning safety without a pretrained encoder. Fails when the shift is large.
  \item Adversarial on top: helps planning only with reach $\times$ headroom; varying $\varepsilon$ extends reach; the adversary's target shifts which shifts are in reach. Reach must be read where the planner reads $h$.
  \item One sentence, test-time alternative: re-estimating normalization statistics on unlabeled shifted frames recovers only the shared offset that jac already removes in training (it lifts sigreg to jac's level under small photometric shifts) and does not help where the latent loses state information (Appendix~\ref{app:bn}).
  \item Detection covers what robustness cannot reach; in-support aliasing remains.
  \item \todo{Limitations: single WM seed on Reacher and for Dubins jac+adv$_h$; CBF single seed; cross-model Dubins CEM protocol-unmatched; purple beyond reach of every training-time method.}
\end{itemize}
