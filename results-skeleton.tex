\section{Results}

\paragraph{Models (naming used throughout).}
\begin{itemize}
  \item sigreg: LeWM default (SIGReg). jac: + encoder-Jacobian Frobenius norm pinned to 1 (sigreg sits at $\approx 10$).
  \item jac+adv$_h$: + adversarial $L_\infty$ ($8/255$, 2 PGD steps) invariance of the safety readout $h$ (MLP adversary head).
  \item jac+adv$_z$: + same adversary, but attack and penalty on the whole latent $\|f(x+\delta)-f(x)\|^2$ (no head).
  \item ``varying radius'': $\varepsilon$ log-uniform in $[4/255, 32/255]$ per training sequence.
  \item Pull variants (CBF section only): jac+pull (predictor action-separation, target 1), jac+pull+adv$_h$.
  \item Margin function $h$: MLP 192-512-512-1 or linear $w^\top z+b$, gradient-penalty margin loss, trained on frozen latents of real + imagined states (real-only is an ablation). $h\ge 0$ = safe.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Planners and protocols.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Raw CEM = LeWM CEM (300 samples, 30 elites; Dubins 30 iters, Reacher 10 iters). CCEM = feasible iff $h>0$ on current + every imagined state; fallback = highest $\min_k h(\hat z_k)$.
  \item Dubins: goals $K$ steps out whose straight line crosses an obstacle; main $K=10$, secondary $K=15$; open loop; 50 eps/seed. OOD: purple obstacles, 90° rotation (test-only).
  \item Reacher: invisible joint-space box; held-out start/goal pairs 25 env steps apart whose recorded path enters the box; frameskip 5, 5-block horizon, replan every 5 blocks; 50 pairs (broad table) or 150 paired episodes (key comparisons, exact McNemar).
  \item Dubins CBF: frozen LeWM $\to$ $h$ $\to$ reachability critic (DDPG in imagination) $\to$ CBF-filtered diffusion policy, 1000 trajectories. Filter needs only current frame + 1 predicted step.
  \item \todo{state seeds per benchmark: Dubins sigreg/jac/jac+pull/jac+pull+adv$_h$ 3 seeds; Dubins jac+adv$_h$ 1 seed; all Reacher models 1 seed (jac s1, varying-radius s1 training)}
\end{itemize}

\begin{itemize}

  \item Why it works: plain invariance. Jacobian pinned to 1 limits how far a pixel change can move the latent; Reacher latent moves 10--20$\times$ less than sigreg's under light / floor 0.2 / white balance. sigreg's damage is mostly a shared affine offset (Table~\ref{tab:bn_baseline}).

\begin{itemize}
  \item Claim (both benchmarks): without a safety term sigreg and jac collide about equally OOD; the red-trained $h$ cuts jac's OOD collisions and does nothing for sigreg.
  \item Dubins $K=10$: CCEM-MLP purple collision jac 14\% vs sigreg 37\% (raw 40 / 37); rotation 14\% vs 48\%. $K=15$: 32 vs 71 (purple), 33 vs 56 (rot.). In distribution CCEM keeps jac at 7--13\%. \todo{paired McNemar at $K=10$; existing: $K=15$ jac beats sigreg on all 3 seed pairs, $p\le 0.024$}.
  \item Reacher: sigreg's $h$ calls everything safe under any photometric shift, so its CCEM \emph{is} raw CEM (identical rows, reacher-results Table 5); jac stays at 0.04--0.08 under every photometric/sensor shift.
  \item Why: CCEM can only veto what $h$ flags. If the encoder moves OOD states across $h$'s boundary, the constraint becomes vacuous (sigreg). If invariance keeps states put, the in-distribution $h$ still applies (jac).
  \item Dynamics shifts (bottom block): no model differs; safety is limited by the planner/world model, not perception. Brief mention only.
  \item Dubins difficulty grows with $K$ (jac purple CCEM collision 14\% $\to$ 32\%); explain via sec.~\ref{sec:limits} (imagined OOD states lose distance information by $\approx 10$ env steps).
  \item \todo{Fig: collision vs.\ goal distance $K\in\{10,15,20\}$ for sigreg / jac, red / purple / rot., raw vs.\ CCEM.}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Summary of when things work}
\begin{itemize}
  \item jac: always helps OOD perception of safety; needed for any safety constraint (CEM or CBF) to survive appearance shift. Fails when the shift is large (incl.\ viewpoint changes).
  \item Adversarial on top: helps planning only with reach $\times$ headroom; varying $\varepsilon$ extends reach; the adversary's target (head vs.\ whole latent) shifts which shifts are in reach, with no uniform winner. On Dubins the fixed-radius head-only variants never help (colour = safety; no shift in band); extended-reach variants untested there.
  \item Pull: neutral in CEM, roughly neutral-to-helpful in the CBF; untuned target.
  \item Detection: shifts beyond reach leave the latent's support and are caught label-free (kNN, clean 99th-percentile threshold); moderate in-support aliasing (camera zoom) is not. Robustness within reach, abstention beyond it.
  \item Test-time BN adaptation: a baseline that matches jac only under small photometric shifts (shared offset, which jac already removes during training); not a substitute.
  \item Imagination depth is the binding limit OOD on Dubins: imagined OOD states lose distance information within $\approx 10$ env steps.
  \item \todo{Limitations: single WM seed on Reacher and for Dubins jac+adv$_h$; CBF single seed; Dubins diamond only in CBF; purple beyond reach of every training-time method (adaptation partly rescues the short-horizon CBF, Appendix~\ref{app:bn}).}
\end{itemize}

  \item CBF: BN halves purple collision ($p<10^{-30}$); the success drop is all unsafe successes, safe success held. Works because the CBF reads 1 predicted step, the depth where BN recovers $h$'s ranking (imagined purple AUC at step 10: 0.45 $\to$ 0.87; step 15: 0.46 $\to$ 0.79).
  \item CEM: at $K=10$ success rises at equal collision; at $K=15$ collision roughly doubles. Unadapted $h$ errs pessimistic on Dubins (vetoes nearly every plan, fallback = stall), and adaptation removes that conservative fallback while long plans still carry no distance information. Rotation is not fixed.
  \item Reacher jac+adv$_h$ + BN: repairs goal reaching under floor shifts (raw CEM progress at floor 0.4: 0.28 $\to$ 0.64) but blurs adv$_h$'s sharper $h$ (projector BatchNorm carries both effects); split planner (adapted goal cost, unadapted $h$) keeps progress (reacher-results Tables 27--30).
